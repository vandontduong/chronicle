# Attention Is All You Need

<p class="meta">1706.03762 · 2017-06 · Vaswani et al.</p>

**Claim.** Sequence transduction does not need recurrence or convolution. Stacked self-attention is enough, and it trains in parallel.

**Why it is on the spine.** Every later GPT/BERT/Gemini-class model is this architecture at larger scale.
