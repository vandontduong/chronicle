## 2015-12 — Nonprofit lab

Charter: AGI that benefits humanity. Musk, Altman, Sutskever, Brockman. Early science is RL plus open tools; the GPT line is already the research program.

- [Introducing OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai/)
- Schulman et al. *PPO*. [arXiv:1707.06347](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347)
- Christiano et al. *Deep RL from human preferences*. [arXiv:1706.03741](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03741)

## 2018–20 — Pretrain, then scale

GPT-1 is generative pre-training. GPT-2 is staged as dual-use. GPT-3 makes in-context learning a method.

- Radford et al. *GPT-1*. [PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/research-covers/language-unsupervised/language_understanding_paper.pdf)
- Radford et al. *GPT-2*. [PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf)
- Brown et al. *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners*. [arXiv:2005.14165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165)

## 2019-07 — Microsoft $1B

Capped-profit entity plus Azure. The lab can train at a scale a nonprofit grant cannot. This is the first deal that changes who pays for the flops.

- [Microsoft — exclusive computing partnership](https://news.microsoft.com/2019/07/22/openai-forms-exclusive-computing-partnership-with-microsoft-to-build-new-azure-ai-supercomputing-technologies/)

## 2022-03 / 2022-11 — InstructGPT, then ChatGPT

RLHF makes a smaller model beat a larger base in human evals. ChatGPT is the same stack with a chat UI — and the event that puts every other lab on a product clock.

- Ouyang et al. *InstructGPT*. [arXiv:2203.02155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155)
- [ChatGPT launch](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt/)

## 2023-01 — Microsoft ~$10B

Exclusive cloud, exclusive IP short of AGI, Copilot as the distribution. OpenAI becomes a product company sitting on one landlord.

- [Microsoft — extending the partnership](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2023/01/23/microsoftandopenaiextendpartnership/)

## 2023-03 — GPT-4

Multimodal, still no recipe. The technical report is a system card with evals.

- OpenAI. *GPT-4 Technical Report*. [arXiv:2303.08774](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08774)
- [GPT-4](https://openai.com/index/gpt-4-research/)

## 2023-11 — Board crisis

Altman fired and restored. Not a methods paper. The scientific shadow is who walks (Leike, Sutskever, then Schulman and Murati). [lab fission]({{ '/timelines/lab-fission/' | relative_url }}).

- [Superalignment](https://openai.com/index/introducing-superalignment/)

## 2024-02 — Sora as a claimed simulator

OpenAI frames a video generator as a world model and does not publish a recipe. The methods argument lives on [world models]({{ '/timelines/world-models/' | relative_url }}).

- [Video generation models as world simulators](https://openai.com/index/video-generation-models-as-world-simulators/)

## 2024-09 — o1

Test-time compute as a product. OpenAI publishes evals and a system card, not the RL recipe. DeepSeek later publishes the cheap public twin.

- [Learning to reason with LLMs](https://openai.com/index/learning-to-reason-with-llms/)
- *o1 System Card*. [arXiv:2412.16720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16720)

## 2025 — SoftBank, Stargate, many landlords

A $40B round led by SoftBank. Stargate with Oracle / SoftBank / MGX as owned compute. CoreWeave and others as extra rack. Microsoft exclusivity on *new* training compute starts to crack. Campus-level debt is on [clouds]({{ '/timelines/clouds/' | relative_url }}).

- [OpenAI — new funding](https://openai.com/index/openai-announces-new-funding/)

## 2025-08 — GPT-5 as one line

The o-series folds into GPT-5. Roadmap beat, not a new architecture: one system that decides when to think. Later 5.x numbers are cadence unless a paper lands.

- [Introducing GPT-5](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5/)

## 2025-10 — PBC recap

OpenAI Group becomes a public-benefit corporation. Microsoft's stake is marked at about $135B (~27% as-converted). OpenAI commits to another $250B of Azure. Microsoft loses right of first refusal on compute. The lab can now buy rack from Amazon and the neoclouds without asking Redmond first.

- [Microsoft — next chapter of the partnership](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/10/28/the-next-chapter-of-the-microsoft-openai-partnership/)

## 2026-02 / 2026-03 — Amazon up to $50B

$15B in preferred now, $35B later on triggers, plus AWS capacity. Seattle now owns paper in both frontier US labs.

- [GeekWire on the Amazon filing](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/filings-how-amazons-50b-openai-deal-actually-works-and-what-theyre-keeping-secret/)
