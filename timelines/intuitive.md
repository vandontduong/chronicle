# Intuitive Surgical

<p class="meta">A console, a wrist, and a disposable. The product is the installed base, not a paper.</p>

SRI built a battlefield telepresence arm. Moll licensed it, named the commercial machine after a painter, and found the market in the pelvis rather than the heart. Structure models that design drugs live on [techbio]({{ '/timelines/techbio/' | relative_url }}). The short-read meter lives on [Illumina]({{ '/timelines/illumina/' | relative_url }}).

## 1995 — License the SRI arm

Fred Moll, John Freund, Robert Younge. Guidant passed. Intuitive takes the SRI telepresence work into a company. The thesis is wristed instruments inside the patient, 3-D vision, and a surgeon who does not stand at the table.

- Green, Hill, Jensen, Shah. *Telepresence Surgery*. IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology, 1995. [IEEE](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/388294)

## 1997–2000 — Mona, then da Vinci, then a ticker

First living-patient cholecystectomy in Belgium (Mona). CE mark and European sales in 1999. FDA clears laparoscopic use in July 2000 after bouncing the file between PMA and 510(k). IPO on Nasdaq as ISRG, 13 June 2000, about $46 million.

- [SEC 10-K history of the 2000 clearance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035267/000089161803002789/f88817a2e10vkza.htm)

## 2001–03 — Prostatectomy becomes the market

FDA clears radical prostatectomy (May 2001). Binder and Kramer publish the early robotic cases. Menon at Henry Ford industrializes the Vattikuti technique and prints the series that other departments copy. The system finds its procedure; cardiac does not.

- Binder and Kramer. *Robotically-assisted laparoscopic radical prostatectomy*. BJU Int. 2001. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11298136/)
- Abbou et al. *Laparoscopic radical prostatectomy with a remote controlled robot*. J Urol. 2001. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11342911/)
- Menon, Tewari, et al. *Robotic radical prostatectomy and the Vattikuti Urology Institute technique*. Urology 2003. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12670612/)

## 2003-06 — Buy the rival

Computer Motion (ZEUS, AESOP) has the earlier NASA-adjacent start and the patent war. Intuitive merges rather than litigate to a standstill. ZEUS is wound down. One sales force, one console family.

- [Forbes, 2005 — $148 million Computer Motion deal](https://www.forbes.com/2005/07/26/surgery-science-medicine-cz_sk_0727surgery.html)

## 2018–19 — Single port, then a lung catheter

da Vinci SP: one cannula. Ion: a shape-sensing catheter for peripheral lung biopsy. The company leaves the abdomen. First-in-human Ion cases; FDA 510(k) K182286 in February 2019.

- Fielding et al. *First Human Use of a New Robotic-Assisted Fiber Optic Sensing Navigation System for Small Peripheral Pulmonary Nodules*. Respiration 2019. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30897583/)

## 2024-03 / 2024-06 — Fifth generation, and a label that cites survival

FDA clears da Vinci 5 (force feedback, more compute on the cart) for the Xi indications except cardiac and pediatric. Separate clearance revises Xi/X labeling for radical prostatectomy using 2007–14 real-world survival — robotic and open look the same at 5–10 years. The argument is no longer only “less blood.”

- [FDA clearance of da Vinci 5](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/intuitive-announces-fda-clearance-of-fifth-generation-robotic-system-da-vinci-5-2024)
- [Revised prostatectomy labeling](https://investor.intuitivesurgical.com/news-releases/news-release-details/intuitive-announces-fda-clearance-revised-da-vinci-xi-and-x/)

## 2025 — Ion as a pathway, not a gadget

NAVIGATE-class electromagnetic work was the prior standard. A 2025 NEJM trial finds navigational bronchoscopy non-inferior to transthoracic needle biopsy with fewer complications. Ion procedure counts: ~54k (2023) → ~96k (2024) → ~144k (2025). Installed base approaching 1,000.

- Lentz et al. *Navigational Bronchoscopy or Transthoracic Needle Biopsy for Lung Nodules*. [NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2414059) (2025)
- [2025 10-K procedure and install figures](https://isrg.gcs-web.com/static-files/f684d492-5169-48b5-9723-8ebcaa05c098)

## 2026-03 — Buy the distributor

~$533 million cash for the ab medica / Abex / Excelencia Robótica da Vinci and Ion distribution business. Italy, Spain, Portugal, Malta, San Marino go direct. Not a technology buy. Control of the channel.

- [8-K, 2 Mar 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035267/000103526726000012/isrg-20260301.htm)
- [MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/intuitive-expands-direct-operations-for-da-vinci-ion-distro-in-europe/)

## What this page is not

A procedure-code list or a Medtronic / CMR / SS Innovations bake-off. New wrists and software land here only if they change what hospitals buy or what the FDA lets the label say. Autonomous suturing demos stay off until a clearance or a trial readout.
