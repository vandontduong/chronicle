# Lab fission

<p class="meta">Where people went, and what thesis they took with them</p>

## 2015-12 — OpenAI founded

Nonprofit research lab. Musk, Altman, Sutskever, Brockman and others. Charter: AGI that benefits humanity.

## 2018 — Musk leaves the OpenAI board

Control and direction fight. Later relevant to xAI.

## 2021-02 — Anthropic founded

Amodei cohort leaves OpenAI. Safety-first frontier lab; later Constitutional AI and Claude.

## 2023-03 — xAI founded

Musk. Grok line. Competes on speed and integration with X.

## 2023-11 — OpenAI board crisis

Altman fired and restored in days. Trust and governance become part of the capability story.

## 2024-06 — Safe Superintelligence (SSI)

Sutskever leaves OpenAI. Thesis: no product until safe superintelligence. Stealth by design.

## 2024-09 / 2025-02 — Thinking Machines Lab

Murati leaves OpenAI; McGrew, Zoph, later Schulman. Product-shaped frontier lab, not SSI's vow of silence.

## 2024–26 — Second wave

Reflection (ex-DeepMind), World Labs, AMI Labs (LeCun), Discovery Loop, Meta Superintelligence Labs. The field is no longer three labs and a long tail.

Dates and valuations move. Treat this page as a map of *theses*, not a cap table.
