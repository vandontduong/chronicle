# SpaceX

<p class="meta">Rockets paid for the plants. Then the plants rented GPUs. Then the ticker bought a coding lab.</p>

This page is the company object — vehicles, constellation, then the AI holding company. Deals that change *who trains* also sit on [lab fission]({{ '/timelines/lab-fission/' | relative_url }}). Landlord math for Colossus sits on [clouds]({{ '/timelines/clouds/' | relative_url }}). There is almost no SpaceX arXiv spine. The record is NASA contracts, FCC filings, S-1 language, and lab blogs.

## 2002 / 2008-09 — Last cash, then orbit

Founded 2002. Falcon 1 fails three times. Fourth flight, 28 Sep 2008, reaches orbit — first privately funded liquid rocket to do so. NASA awards Commercial Resupply shortly after. The firm that later lists at a trillion-dollar print almost dies on flight three.

- [NASA on Falcon 1 / early COTS](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/commercial-orbital-transportation-services-cots/)

## 2010–17 — Falcon 9, then the booster comes back

Falcon 9 first flight 2010. Dragon cargo berths at ISS 2012 — first private spacecraft to do that. 21 Dec 2015: first-stage landing at Cape Canaveral after an orbital mission. 2017: first *reflown* booster (SES-10). Reuse is the product. Launch price and cadence become a cash engine, not a science project.

- [NASA Commercial Resupply](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/commercial-space/commercial-resupply-services/)

## 2018-02 — Falcon Heavy

Three cores, Tesla Roadster as dummy payload. Heavy-lift that is not Shuttle and not SLS. Still a Falcon family vehicle. Starship is the intended replacement, not a second product line that ships.

## 2019-05 — Starlink as a product

First batch of 60 operational satellites, 23 May 2019. Consumer terminals follow. By the mid-2020s Starlink is most of *revenue*; Falcon is most of *mass to orbit*. The constellation is why orbital compute is even a sentence SpaceX can write in an S-1.

- [Starlink](https://www.starlink.com)

## 2020-05 — Crew Dragon Demo-2

Hurley and Behnken, 30 May 2020. First private company to put humans in orbit; first crewed orbital launch from US soil since Shuttle. Crew-1 that November is the operational NASA service. Human spaceflight is now a line item, not a demo.

- [NASA Demo-2](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/space-x-demo-2/)

## 2021-04 — HLS

NASA picks SpaceX for the Artemis III human landing system (Starship HLS). Option later for Artemis IV. The Moon contract is a Starship *requirement*, not a Falcon mission. It is also a second public customer next to ISS crew.

- [NASA HLS selection](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-selects-spacex-to-return-americans-to-moon/)

## 2021–24 — Starshield

Classified cousin of Starlink: encrypted government comms and, separately, an NRO proliferated-sensing contract reported in the low billions. Ukraine terminals later get a Starshield beam. Defense cash sits next to consumer broadband. Not a new rocket.

- Reuters later reporting on the NRO constellation (search “SpaceX NRO Starshield”) — cite the outlet when you quote a dollar figure; the contract itself is classified.

## 2023-04 — Starship integrated flight

IFT-1, Boca Chica, 20 Apr 2023. Vehicle is destroyed. Later IFT flights reach the intended velocity and, eventually, catch attempts. This page does not keep a flight log. The beat is: the heavy-lift that HLS and Starmind both assume has left the slide deck.

- [SpaceX Starship](https://www.spacex.com/vehicles/starship/)

## 2023-07 — xAI as a separate lab

Musk incorporates the anti-OpenAI lab after leaving the OpenAI board years earlier. Thesis: a truth-seeking model, trained outside the Bay safety stack. Colossus in Memphis is built for Grok, not for Starlink.

- [xAI announcement](https://x.ai/blog/about)
- Also on: [lab fission]({{ '/timelines/lab-fission/' | relative_url }})

## 2024-03 — Grok-1 weights

314B MoE, 25% active, Apache-2. First large open weight from the Musk stack. Not a capability frontier paper. It is a distribution beat: the lab will sometimes ship weights.

- [xAI: open release](https://x.ai/blog/grok-os)
- [Weights on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/xai-org/grok-1)

## 2024-08 / 2025-02 — Grok-2, then Grok-3 on Colossus

Grok-2 is the first xAI model that looks like a product API rather than a demo. Grok-3 (Feb 2025) is trained on the Memphis cluster; xAI cites ~10× the pretraining compute of Grok-2 and ships a reasoning (“Think”) variant. Later Grok-4 / code-fast SKUs are version logs — they stay off this page unless a paper or a deal changes who trains.

- [Grok-2](https://x.ai/news/grok-2)
- [Grok-3](https://x.ai/news/grok-3)

## 2024-11 / 2025-09 — Direct-to-cell, then spectrum

FCC grants Supplemental Coverage from Space for Starlink-to-unmodified phones (T-Mobile partnership). Jan 2024 birds already carried the payload. Sep 2025: SpaceX agrees to take EchoStar spectrum — reported **$17B** cash-and-stock — so the service is not only a partner’s leftover band. Consumer broadband becomes a phone SKU.

- [Ars Technica on the FCC order](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2024/11/fcc-approves-starlink-plan-for-cellular-phone-service-with-some-limits/)
- [TechCrunch on EchoStar](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/09/why-spacex-made-a-17b-bet-on-the-direct-to-cell-market/)

## 2025-03 — xAI acquires X

All-stock. Musk: xAI at $80B, X at $33B ($45B EV less $12B debt). Distribution (the feed) and the model sit in one holding company. Training data and product surface are no longer a commercial negotiation.

- [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/29/elon-musk-says-xai-acquired-x/)

## 2024–26 — Colossus 1 and 2

Memphis / Southaven training campus. SpaceX S-1 later cites ~**1 GW** nameplate across the pair, turbines then a permanent plant. Built for Grok; spare capacity becomes a rental product.

Dollars and kill-switches: [clouds]({{ '/timelines/clouds/' | relative_url }}).

## 2026-02 — SpaceX acquires xAI

Share exchange. Boards: SpaceX ~$1T, xAI ~$250B. Combined ~$1.25T private. Musk memo: vertically integrated engine — AI, rockets, space internet, X — and **data centers in space** as a stated driver. xAI holders get 0.1433 SpaceX shares per xAI share.

- [NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/02/business/spacex-xai-deal.html)
- [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/musks-spacex-merge-with-xai-combined-valuation-125-trillion-bloomberg-news-2026-02-02/)
- [WSJ on the structure](https://www.wsj.com/tech/elon-musk-xai-spacex-merger-2896ae1e)

## 2026-04 / 2026-06 / 2026-08 — Cursor

April: option — buy Anysphere for **$60B** in stock *or* pay ~$10B to keep working together. June 16: option exercised, days after the IPO; close targeted Q3. August: deal closed; Grok Bot ships on the combined stack. Cursor is the first large *outside* lab purchase. Coding distribution that Grok did not win on its own.

- [The Verge, exercise](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/950571/spacex-is-officially-buying-cursor-for-60-billion)
- [NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/16/business/spacex-cursor-aquisition-ipo.html)
- Also on: [lab fission]({{ '/timelines/lab-fission/' | relative_url }}), [agents]({{ '/timelines/agents/' | relative_url }})

## 2026-05 / 2026-06 — Anthropic and Google rent Colossus

Anthropic: full Colossus 1, ~300 MW / 200k+ GPUs, S-1 rate **$1.25B/month** through May 2029 with a short kill switch. Google: ~110k GPUs, **$920M/month** Oct 2026–Jun 2029, framed as Gemini Enterprise bridge capacity. Reflection takes a smaller Colossus 2 slice later.

This is SpaceX as landlord, not as GPU-ABS borrower. Full writeup: [clouds]({{ '/timelines/clouds/' | relative_url }}).

- [WIRED on the S-1 Anthropic dollars](https://www.wired.com/story/spacex-ipo-anthropic-compute-finances-risks/)
- [Reuters on Google](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/spacex-signs-cloud-deal-with-google-2026-06-05/)
- [WSJ Anthropic announcement](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/anthropic-inks-deal-to-use-all-of-spacexs-colossus-1-compute-capacity-56a7e2a1)

## 2026-06 — IPO

Nasdaq SPCX. Record raise (~$75B at $135), ~$1.77T at pricing. Whole company lists — Starlink is not carved out. S-1 language: dual monetization of compute (train Grok *and* rent the cluster). AI capex already rivaling the space division in the trailing quarters.

- [Reuters on pricing](https://www.reuters.com/world/musks-spacex-prices-record-75-billion-ipo-135-share-2026-06-11/)

## 2026 — Starmind (orbital compute)

FCC filing earlier in 2026 for up to **one million** AI satellites. Public name: Starmind. AI1 birds specified ~120 kW average compute, ~600 km, Starship-class deploy. Reporting: Nvidia Vera/Rubin as a first payload partner. Prototypes talked for 2027. This is a thesis, not a fleet. Treat it as the *reason given* for folding xAI into SpaceX, not as capacity you can rent today.

- [Notebookcheck on AI1](https://www.notebookcheck.net/SpaceX-lists-AI1-satellite-cooling-specs-for-Starmind-data-center-in-space.1340218.0.html)
- [Nvidia payload reporting](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/spacex-nvidia-starmind-ai1-compute-payload)
