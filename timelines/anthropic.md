# Anthropic

<p class="meta">A frontier lab whose alignment method is part of the product. Amazon and Google write the cheques. Claude is the distribution.</p>

The preference papers also live on [post-training]({{ '/timelines/post-training/' | relative_url }}). RSP, sleeper agents, and many-shot jailbreaks live on [safety]({{ '/timelines/safety/' | relative_url }}). The Amodei fork lives on [lab fission]({{ '/timelines/lab-fission/' | relative_url }}). Cloud offtakes live on [clouds]({{ '/timelines/clouds/' | relative_url }}).

## 2021-02 — Founded

Amodei cohort leaves OpenAI. Thesis: helpful-honest-harmless as the evaluation frame, then a written constitution instead of an unlabeled RLHF stew.

- [Anthropic — company](https://www.anthropic.com/company)
- Askell et al. *A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment*. [arXiv:2112.00861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.00861)
- Bai et al. *Training a Helpful and Harmless Assistant with RLHF*. [arXiv:2204.05862](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.05862)

## 2022-12 — Constitutional AI

Harmlessness from AI feedback plus a constitution you can read. This is the scientific payload of the fork. Later Claude versions are this method plus scale.

- Bai et al. *Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback*. [arXiv:2212.08073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback)
- [Claude's constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claudes-constitution)

## 2023-03 / 2023-07 — Claude, then Claude 2

First public assistant. Constitutional training is the differentiator they will sell to enterprises that will not touch OpenAI.

- [Introducing Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude)
- [Claude 2](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-2)

## 2023-09 / 2023-10 — Amazon $4B, Google ~$2B

Two landlords. AWS is the training home; Google TPUs are the second rack. The pattern copies Microsoft–OpenAI without giving either cloud exclusivity forever.

- [Anthropic + Amazon](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-amazon)
- [Amazon — $4B](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-invests-in-anthropic)
- [Anthropic + Google](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-google)

## 2024-03 — Claude 3 family

Opus / Sonnet / Haiku. Frontier on several public benches; the product is a three-tier API, not one chat window.

- [Claude 3](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-3-family)

## 2024-05 / 2024-10 — Scaling monosemanticity; computer use

Dictionary learning on a production model — interpretability as a lab paper, not a safety slide. Computer use puts Claude on a desktop. That loop continues on [agents]({{ '/timelines/agents/' | relative_url }}).

- [Scaling Monosemanticity](https://www.anthropic.com/research/mapping-mind-language-model)
- [Introducing computer use](https://www.anthropic.com/news/3-5-models-and-computer-use)

## 2024-11 — Amazon another $4B; MCP

Second Amazon cheque. Model Context Protocol is the unsexy beat: a socket so agents stop being one-off harnesses.

- [Amazon — additional investment](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-anthropic-ai-investment)
- [Model Context Protocol](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol)

## 2025-05 — Claude 4

Opus 4 / Sonnet 4. Long-horizon coding becomes the scoreboard they want to own. Later 4.x and 5.x drops are cadence; system cards stay the primary cite.

- [Claude Opus 4 and Sonnet 4](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-4)
- [System cards](https://www.anthropic.com/system-cards)

## 2025-11 — Microsoft $5B, Nvidia up to $10B, $30B Azure

The other Seattle lab now writes a cheque too. Anthropic commits tens of billions of Azure + Nvidia rack. Circular in the same way as OpenAI–Microsoft–Nvidia; different tenant.

- [Forbes, 18 Nov 2025](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2025/11/18/2025s-ai-spending-frenzy-continues-anthropic-inks-30-billion-cloud-computing-deal-with-nvidia-microsoft/)
