# Stripe

<p class="meta">Payments API → money OS for internet firms → rails for agents and tokens. Almost no papers.</p>

This page is the firm that settles the AI economy. Agent *loops* live on [agents]({{ '/timelines/agents/' | relative_url }}). Instant Checkout as an OpenAI product surface is pointed from [OpenAI]({{ '/timelines/openai/' | relative_url }}). Hash-to-GPU sheds stay on [cryptocurrency]({{ '/timelines/cryptocurrency/' | relative_url }}); GPU landlords on [clouds]({{ '/timelines/clouds/' | relative_url }}).

## 2011-09 — Seven lines, public

Patrick and John Collison ship a payments API after a private beta. The product is not a new card network. It is the bank and risk stack hidden behind a few lines of code, priced 2.9% + $0.30, no monthly fee. Developer distribution, not a merchant-account sales force.

- [Stripe — About](https://stripe.com/about)

## 2012 — Connect

One business taking a card is solved. Platforms need split, hold, and pay out (Shopify, Lyft). Connect makes Stripe the money layer *inside someone else's product*. Later AI companies inherit the same primitive: the lab is a platform with connected sellers, usage, and payouts.

- [Stripe Connect](https://stripe.com/connect)

## 2016 — Atlas, then Radar

Atlas incorporates a Delaware company from anywhere so the founder can take a card before they have a US bank. Radar is the first Stripe product that is a model: fraud scores trained on the network, not a rules engine the merchant writes. No paper. The object that changes is who gets to start, and which charges die.

- [Stripe Atlas](https://stripe.com/atlas)
- [Stripe Radar](https://stripe.com/radar)

## 2018 — Billing as the recurring SKU

Subscriptions, invoices, later meters. Issuing and Terminal land the same year (cards out, hardware in). Billing is the product the token companies already run on before anyone names agentic commerce.

- [Stripe Billing](https://stripe.com/billing)

## 2025-02 — Bridge

Stripe completes the acquisition of Bridge, a stablecoin orchestration layer (issue, hold, on/off-ramp). Fiat checkout stays. Cross-border and machine-speed settlement get a second rail. Stripe does not state a price on the close note.

- [Stripe completes Bridge acquisition](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-completes-bridge-acquisition)

## 2025-07 / 2025-09 — Privy, then Tempo

July: Privy (embedded wallets; Stripe later cites 110 million). September: Tempo, a payments L1 incubated with Paradigm — dedicated lanes, sub-second finality. Wallet plus chain, still not a model lab.

- [2025 annual letter / tender](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-2025-update)

## 2025-09 — Agentic Commerce Protocol

OpenAI ships Instant Checkout in ChatGPT (US Etsy first). Stripe co-releases ACP, Apache 2.0: agent, merchant, and payer share a checkout language. Shared Payment Tokens pass a credential without handing the agent the card. The protocol home is here; the chat surface is [OpenAI]({{ '/timelines/openai/' | relative_url }}).

- [Stripe — Instant Checkout and ACP](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-openai-instant-checkout)
- [Developing an open standard for agentic commerce](https://stripe.com/blog/developing-an-open-standard-for-agentic-commerce)

## 2025-12 — Agentic Commerce Suite

ACP is a spec. The Suite is one integration for catalog, discovery, checkout, and fraud across agents. Coach, Kate Spade, URBN, Etsy, Wix, and peers onboard so they do not build a connector per model vendor.

- [Agentic Commerce Suite](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/agentic-commerce-suite)

## 2026-01 — Metronome

Close of Metronome, usage-billing orchestration already used by OpenAI, Anthropic, and NVIDIA (Stripe's words). Metering becomes a Stripe SKU instead of a side system. Price unstated on the close note.

- [Stripe completes Metronome acquisition](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-completes-metronome-acquisition)

## 2026-03 — Machine Payments Protocol

MPP, co-authored with Tempo: an agent pays an API over HTTP — no checkout page. Micropayments, recurring, stablecoin settlement into the existing Stripe balance. The loop on [agents]({{ '/timelines/agents/' | relative_url }}) can now spend.

- [Introducing the Machine Payments Protocol](https://stripe.com/blog/machine-payments-protocol)
- [MPP docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/machine/mpp)

## 2026-04 — Sessions: buyers and builders

288 product and feature launches under one sentence: economic infrastructure for AI. Load-bearing SKUs: Link wallets for agents (one-time cards, human approve); Agentic Commerce Suite onto Google AI Mode / Gemini (after OpenAI, Microsoft, Meta); Stripe Projects so an agent can provision accounts and infra from a CLI; streaming payments = Metronome meters + Tempo settlement; Radar aimed at token and trial abuse. Not a foundation-model company. A settlement and metering company sitting next to one.

- [Sessions 2026 newsroom](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/sessions-2026)
- [Everything announced at Sessions 2026](https://stripe.com/blog/everything-we-announced-at-sessions-2026)

## 2026-08 — OpenRouter agreed

Stripe agrees to acquire OpenRouter, a gateway that routes across 400+ models from 80+ providers. Official note: agreed, not closed; no dollar figure from Stripe. The intended object is one pipe for *which model ran* and *what it cost* (OpenRouter + Metronome), not a new weight.

- [Stripe agrees to acquire OpenRouter](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-agrees-to-acquire-openrouter)

## What this page is not

A Sessions changelog, a valuation diary, or a card-network history. Radar model cards stay off until they publish one. x402 and other open pay-by-HTTP specs belong here only if Stripe's object changes. GPU offtakes stay on [clouds]({{ '/timelines/clouds/' | relative_url }}).
