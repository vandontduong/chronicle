# Essays

<p class="meta">Named frames. The sentence people still use, not the paper dump.</p>

This page is the argument as an essay. Architectures live on the [capability stack]({{ '/timelines/capability-stack/' | relative_url }}). What may rewrite itself lives on [RSI]({{ '/timelines/rsi/' | relative_url }}). Capability per flop lives on [efficiency]({{ '/timelines/efficiency/' | relative_url }}). The crawl lives on [data]({{ '/timelines/data/' | relative_url }}). The imitation game as a *test* lives on [evals]({{ '/timelines/evals/' | relative_url }}).

A paper may sit on two pages. The essay sits here.

## 1950 — Imitation as the test

Turing: a digital computer can be said to think if it can play the imitation game; the usual objections fail. Learning machines, not encoded expertise. The field still argues inside this frame.

- Turing. *Computing Machinery and Intelligence*. Mind 59 (236). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/LIX.236.433)
- What followed as a *scoreboard*: [evals]({{ '/timelines/evals/' | relative_url }}).

## 1965 — The explosion sentence

Good: an ultraintelligent machine can design a better one; that is an intelligence explosion; it may be the last invention — if the machine stays docile. The monograph then wanders into brain theory. The sentence is what survived.

- Good. *Speculations Concerning the First Ultraintelligent Machine*. Advances in Computers, 1965. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2458%2808%2960418-0)
- What followed as a *loop*: [RSI]({{ '/timelines/rsi/' | relative_url }}).

## 2009 — Messy data beats a neat theory

Halevy, Norvig, Pereira: language tasks do not look like F = ma. A trillion dirty web n-grams beat a million clean Brown-Corpus words. Embrace the complexity; the data is the ally.

- Halevy, Norvig, Pereira. *The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Data*. IEEE Intelligent Systems, 2009. [Google Research](https://research.google/blog/the-unreasonable-effectiveness-of-data/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/MIS.2009.36)
- What followed as a *mix*: [data]({{ '/timelines/data/' | relative_url }}).

## 2017-11 — Weights are the program

Karpathy: a trained net is not another tool in the ML box. It is Software 2.0 — a program written in weights, compiled by gradient descent. Constant-time eval, silicon that wants one activation.

- Karpathy. *Software 2.0*. 11 Nov 2017. [Medium](https://karpathy.medium.com/software-2-0-a64152b37c35)
- What followed as a *stack*: [capability stack]({{ '/timelines/capability-stack/' | relative_url }}).

## 2019-03 — The bitter lesson

Sutton: methods that bake in how researchers think they think help, then plateau. Search and learning ride falling compute and win. The name plus the four-step diagnosis. Moore as the *ultimate* cause is the overreach; the direction of chess, speech, and Go holds.

- Sutton. *The Bitter Lesson*. 13 Mar 2019. [incompleteideas.net](http://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html)
- What followed as a *ratio*: [efficiency]({{ '/timelines/efficiency/' | relative_url }}). As architecture: [capability stack]({{ '/timelines/capability-stack/' | relative_url }}).

## 2020-05 — Scale as a plan

Gwern: once the architecture is uniform enough, bigger nets on simple objectives grow new abilities. GPT-3 is the exhibit. Sutton named the bitterness; this named the *bet*.

- Branwen. *The Scaling Hypothesis*. 28 May 2020. [gwern.net](https://gwern.net/scaling-hypothesis)
- Laws and GPT-3 as papers: [capability stack]({{ '/timelines/capability-stack/' | relative_url }}).

## 2020-08 — The hardware lottery

Hooker: an idea often wins because it fits the hardware and software of the moment, not because it is the better idea. Specialization makes leaving the GPU path expensive. The counter-move to Sutton.

- Hooker. *The Hardware Lottery*. Aug 2020. [essay](https://hardwarelottery.github.io/) · [arXiv:2009.06489](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.06489)
- What followed as *silicon*: [Nvidia]({{ '/timelines/nvidia/' | relative_url }}), [integrated circuits]({{ '/timelines/integrated-circuits/' | relative_url }}).

## 2024-09 — The Intelligence Age

Altman: deep learning worked, got predictably better with scale, and the labs spent more. Superintelligence in a few thousand days is the stated bet. The constraint he names is compute, energy, and will — not a new architecture. Lab voice, not a field diagnosis.

- Altman. *The Intelligence Age*. 23 Sep 2024. [ia.samaltman.com](https://ia.samaltman.com/)
- Firm page: [OpenAI]({{ '/timelines/openai/' | relative_url }}). Rack: [clouds]({{ '/timelines/clouds/' | relative_url }}).

## 2024-10 — Machines of loving grace

Amodei: if powerful AI is handled, biology, mind, poverty, and governance compress decades into years. “Country of geniuses in a datacenter.” Conditional utopia. Rates are stipulated, not measured.

- Amodei. *Machines of Loving Grace*. Oct 2024. [darioamodei.com](https://darioamodei.com/machines-of-loving-grace)
- Firm page: [Anthropic]({{ '/timelines/anthropic/' | relative_url }}).
