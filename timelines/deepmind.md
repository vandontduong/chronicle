# DeepMind

<p class="meta">Games → science · highest scientific yield of any single lab line</p>

## 2013-12 — DQN on Atari

One network learns many games from pixels. Proof that deep RL is a research program, not a trick.

- Paper: [Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning]({{ '/papers/1312.5602/' | relative_url }}) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5602) · [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1312.5602)

## 2016-03 — AlphaGo beats Lee Sedol

Policy + value nets and MCTS. Public shock; RL talent floods the field.

## 2017-10 — AlphaGo Zero / AlphaZero

Self-play without human games. Tabula rasa becomes a slogan and a method.

- Paper: [Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.01815) · [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1712.01815)

## 2019-11 — MuZero

Learns the rules. Planning with a learned model, not a given simulator.

- Paper: [Mastering Atari, Go, Chess and Shogi by Planning with a Learned Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08265) · [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1911.08265)

## 2020-11 / 2021 — AlphaFold 2

Protein structure at useful accuracy. First DeepMind result that changes a natural science.

## 2022-05 — Gato

One generalist agent, many tasks. Uneven, but the lab's language shifts from games to general agents.

- Paper: [A Generalist Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06175) · [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2205.06175)

## 2023–26 — Gemini line

DeepMind + Brain merged into Google DeepMind. Frontier multimodal models; agents and long context become the product surface.
